About

Choi Jong-In is one of the most powerful S-Rank hunters in South Korea and is widely recognized as the country's greatest mage. He serves as the Guild Master of the Illusion Guild, one of Korea's top hunter guilds. His reputation as a fire magic specialist is unmatched, and his name is well known across the entire hunter community both in Korea and internationally.

Despite his enormous fame and power, Choi Jong-In is a reasonable and professional individual who genuinely cares about protecting people. He is one of the few top hunters who recognizes Jin-Woo's extraordinary growth early on and treats him with respect rather than dismissiveness.

Powers & Abilities

Choi Jong-In specializes in fire magic, making him one of the most destructive long-range fighters among all Korean hunters. His flames are powerful enough to devastate entire groups of monsters in a single attack, and his control over fire magic is precise and highly refined. As an S-Rank mage, his mana reserves and magical output far surpass any lower ranked hunter, making him a fearsome force on the battlefield.

Personality

Choi Jong-In carries himself with the calm confidence of someone who has earned their reputation through real experience. He is composed, professional, and level-headed even in dangerous situations. Unlike some powerful hunters who let their status go to their heads, he remains grounded and approachable. He is also perceptive, quick to recognize true strength in others, which is why he was one of the first to acknowledge just how special Sung Jin-Woo truly is.
