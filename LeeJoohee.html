About

Lee Joo-hee is a B-Rank healer and one of the first people to fight alongside Sung Jin-Woo. She was part of the same raid team as Jin-Woo during the terrifying double dungeon incident ,the event that changed Jin-Woo's life forever. Surviving that nightmare left a deep mark on her, and she never forgot the kindness Jin-Woo showed her during that raid.

After the double dungeon incident, Joo-hee became deeply shaken by the dangers of being a hunter. The trauma of nearly dying pushed her to question whether she should continue raiding at all. Despite this, her bond with Jin-Woo and her caring nature kept her connected to the hunter world.

Powers & Abilities

Lee Joo-hee's main ability is healing , she can restore the health and injuries of her teammates during and after battles. As a B-Rank hunter, her healing magic is reliable and effective, making her a valuable support member in any raid team. Although she is not a fighter, her ability to keep teammates alive makes her presence on the battlefield extremely important.

Personality

Joo-hee is warm, kind, and genuinely caring toward the people around her. She is one of the few people who treated Jin-Woo with respect and concern even when he was considered the weakest hunter. Her gentle and empathetic nature makes her easy to like, and her emotional honesty sets her apart from many of the more hardened hunters in the story.
