About

Cha Hae-in is one of the most powerful S-Rank hunters in South Korea and a top member of the Hunters Guild. She is widely recognized as Korea's number one female hunter, known for her exceptional swordsmanship and calm presence on the battlefield.

Unlike most hunters, Cha Hae-in has a unique sensitivity to mana , she can smell the odor that other hunters emit from their mana, which she finds unpleasant. However, Sung Jin-Woo is the only hunter whose mana she finds different, which first draws her attention to him.

Powers & Abilities

Cha Hae-in is an incredibly skilled swordsman whose speed and precision in combat place her among the very best hunters in the world. Her sword techniques are fast, powerful, and elegant, allowing her to take down monsters far larger than herself with ease. As an S-Rank hunter, her physical strength and reflexes are far beyond any ordinary human.

Personality

Cha Hae-in is quiet, serious, and highly professional. She does not socialize much and tends to keep to herself. However, beneath her cold exterior, she is a deeply caring person who is loyal to those she trusts. As she grows closer to Sung Jin-Woo, her warmer and more gentle side gradually begins to show.
